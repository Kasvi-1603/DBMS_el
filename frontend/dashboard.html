Fraud networks

Connected components of claims that share a garage, doctor, vehicle, witness, customer record, phone or address — traced with a recursive SQL query until no further link exists. A shared attribute is evidence of association, not proof of fraud; a large network is not automatically suspicious.

Risk scoring

Four weighted signals combine into a single 0–100 score. None of them alone is proof of fraud — together, they prioritize where an investigator should look first.

Composite score, by factor

Points per factor, each rounded to 0.1. The score is the exact sum of the four factor points.

Claim / premium ratio
35 × min(claim ÷ premium ÷ 8, 1). Missing premium → 0 points.
35 pts
Network size
25 × min((claims in connected component − 1) ÷ 5, 1)
25 pts
Garage frequency
20 × min(other claims using the same garage ÷ 5, 1)
20 pts
Category deviation
20 × min(max((amount − category avg) ÷ avg, 0) ÷ 2, 1). Needs ≥ 5 claims in the category.
20 pts
